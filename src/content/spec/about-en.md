> Here is Levi's personal introduction and an overview of what this blog covers.

Hello, I'm Levi—a technology enthusiast who enjoys exploring web tools, open-source projects, and personal productivity workflows. This blog serves as a long-term record of my learning, hands-on experiments, and troubleshooting notes, turning scattered experience into clear, reproducible guides and references.

##### Blog Topics

- Web development, Astro, Jekyll, GitHub Pages, and frontend toolchain practices.
- Daily developer tooling notes covering Git, VS Code, terminal workflows, and automation scripts.
- Practical infrastructure notes on servers, reverse proxies, SSL/TLS certificates, domains, and basic operations.
- Hands-on experiences with digital tools, writing software, productivity methods, and personal knowledge management.

##### Content Principles

Content on this site focuses primarily on original, firsthand records. I aim to document the problem context, actionable steps, key configurations, errors encountered, and verified fixes as clearly as possible. Whenever third-party references, projects, or external images are cited, their sources or intended purposes are noted in the article.

Rather than attempting to cover every concept exhaustively, these posts emphasize "what I actually built, what pitfalls came up, and how they were resolved." If you spot outdated or inaccurate information in any article, feel free to reach out through the contact channels below.

##### Acknowledgements

This site is built on an open-source blog theme and continuously customized for my own workflow. Thanks to the open-source community for the tools, documentation, and inspiration.
